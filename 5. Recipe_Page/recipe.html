Grandma's Secret dhokla

This Dhokla recipe has been in my family for generations and never fails to delight.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of fine gram flour
  • 1/2 cup of yogurt
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 tsp of baking soda
  • Fresh chilies, ginger, and coriander for aroma and taste
  • sugar and salt to enhance flavor

Instruction

  1. Sift the gram flour to remove lumps and aerate it.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine the gram flour, yogurt, and water. Mix until just combined, avoiding overmixing.
  3. Add the leavening agent (like Eno fruit salt) just before steaming.
  4. Grease a cake tin and pour the batter into it.
  5. Place the tin in a steamer and steam on medium heat for about 15 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Heat oil in a small pan, add mustard seeds, sesame seeds, curry leaves, and slit green chilies.
  7. Pour the tempering over the steamed dhokla and garnish with chopped coriander leaves and grated coconut.
  8. Serve warm with coriander chutney or any chutney of your choice. Enjoy this light and fluffy delicacy as a snack or part of your breakfast spread.
